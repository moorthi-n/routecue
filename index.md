# RouteCue Support

RouteCue creates private, offline photo breadcrumbs that help users retrace confusing indoor routes.

## How RouteCue Works

1. Start a new trail.
2. Photograph important landmarks and turns.
3. When returning, follow the photos in reverse order.
4. End the trail when you reach your destination.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does RouteCue track my location?

No. RouteCue does not use GPS or track your location.

### Does RouteCue require an internet connection?

No. RouteCue is designed to work offline.

### Where are my route photos stored?

Route photos are stored locally on your device.

### Do I need to create an account?

No account is required to use RouteCue.
