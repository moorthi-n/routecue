# RouteCue

## Your visual path back—when GPS can’t help.

GPS can bring you to a building, but it may not remember the parking level, elevator, hallway, entrance, or turns you used inside.

RouteCue helps you retrace confusing indoor routes using simple visual breadcrumbs.

## See It. Save It. Follow It Back.

As you walk, capture photos of recognizable landmarks and important turns. When you are ready to return, RouteCue presents your photos in reverse order—one visual cue at a time.

No complicated maps. No directions to memorize. Just the landmarks you actually saw along the way.

## How RouteCue Works

1. **Start a trail** before entering a confusing space.
2. **Capture landmarks** such as elevators, signs, hallways, doors, floors, and turns.
3. **Begin your return** when you are ready to go back.
4. **Follow each photo** in reverse order.
5. **Complete the trail** when you reach your destination.

## Useful in Everyday Places

RouteCue can help you retrace your steps through:

* Multilevel parking garages
* Airports and transit centers
* Hospitals and medical complexes
* Hotels and resorts
* Cruise ships
* Shopping malls
* College and corporate campuses
* Convention centers and event venues
* Other large indoor spaces where GPS is less useful

## More Than a Parking Pin

A parking pin may show where your vehicle is located, but it doesn’t always show how to navigate the floors, elevators, hallways, entrances, and turns between you and your car.

RouteCue records the visual path—not just the final destination.

## Private by Design

RouteCue is designed to work:

* Without an account
* Without location tracking
* Without map data
* Without advertising trackers
* Without an internet connection

Your visual trails remain on your device, giving you a private and practical way to remember the route back.

## Made for Real-Life Navigation Confusion

Whether you are traveling, visiting a large hospital, attending a convention, staying at a resort, or simply trying to find your car, RouteCue provides a reassuring visual reminder of the path you took.

**GPS gets you there. RouteCue helps you remember the way back.**
