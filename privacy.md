# RouteCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

RouteCue is designed with privacy in mind.

## Information We Collect

RouteCue does not require an account and does not collect personal information, location data, analytics data, or advertising identifiers.

## Route Photos

Photos captured within RouteCue are used to create visual breadcrumb trails. These photos are stored locally on the user's device and are not transmitted to RouteCue or any external server.

## Internet and Third-Party Services

RouteCue does not require an internet connection and does not use third-party advertising or tracking services.

## Data Deletion

Users can delete their trails and associated photos from within the app. Removing the app also removes locally stored RouteCue data from the device, subject to the device’s backup settings.

## Children’s Privacy

RouteCue does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when RouteCue’s functionality or privacy practices change. Updates will be posted on this page.
